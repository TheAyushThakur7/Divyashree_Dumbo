The Monologue

Read carefully... every word carries the weight of my truth.

01 Recognizing Your Silence

"Main jaanta hoon, Divya... ki jab tum aaj meri taraf dekhti ho, toh tumhein mere chehre mein woh insaan nazar nahi aata jisse tumne kabhi beintehaa mohabbat ki thi. Tumhein bas woh shaks yaad aata hai jisne apne hi haathon se tumhare bharose ka katal kiya, jisne tumhare masoom se dil ko itne ghutne-tekne wale zakhm diye ki aakhirkaar tumne mehsoos karna hi chhod diya. Aur sach kahoon? Sabse zyada takleef is baat ki nahi hai ki tum mujhse naaraz ho... takleef yeh hai ki ab tumhein mujhse nafrat bhi nahi hoti. Tumhare andar sab kuch sun ho chuka hai."

02 Understanding Your Armor

"Main samajhta hoon ki tumne apne ird-gird yeh lohe ki deewarein kyun khadi ki hain. Tumhara yeh sannata, yeh patthar jaisa roop, yeh har baat par shaq karna... yeh sab tumhari fitrat nahi, balki un zakhmon ke nishaan hain jo is zalim ne khud tumhein diye the. Tumne apne dil ko apni marzi se pathar nahi banaya, Divya; tumne use dobara kisi ke haath se tutne se bachane ke liye khud kaid kiya hai. Aur aaj agar tum mere saamne khadi hokar yeh kehti ho ki tumhein koi fark nahi padta, ki ab tumhein kuch mehsoos nahi hota—toh main tumse yeh haq bhi kaise chheen sakta hoon? Tumhein is kaid tak laane wala koi aur nahi, main khud tha."

03 No Entitlement, Only Penance

"Lekin ek baat hai... main yahan apne purane haq jataane nahi aaya, na hi tumse woh be-panah mohabbat bheek mein maangne aaya hoon jise sambhaalne ki aukat kabhi meri thi hi nahi. Main jaanta hoon ki chand khoobsurat alfaaz, kuch rote hue waade ya yeh rom-com wali baatein tumhare guzre hue dard ka ek aansu tak nahi mita saktiyan. Tumhara bharosa koi aisi sasti cheez nahi jo mere maangne se laut aaye. Use toh mujhe apne har aane wale din mein, apni har saans ki keemat par kamaana hoga."

04 This Trial is My Crucible

"Yeh jo kuch hafte tumne mujhe diye hain, Divya, mere liye koi aam 'chance' nahi hain. Yeh meri zindagi ka sabse bada imtihaan hain. Main tumhare har ek shaq ko sunna chahta hoon, har us cheekh ka saamna karna chahta hoon jo tumne akele kamre mein ro-ro kar dabayi hongi. Is baar main apni safai dene nahi, tumhara woh dard apnane aaya hoon jo maine tumhe diya tha. Aur agar in chand hafton ke baad bhi tumhara yeh dil mere liye na dhadka... agar tumhein lagta hai ki main tab bhi wahi purana gunehgaar hoon, toh main tumhare faisle ko chupchap sar aankhon par rakhunga. Main tumhein dobara kisi kasoorvaar ki tarah mehsoos nahi karaunga, kyunki tumhara mujhe ek mauka dena tumhara ehsaan hai, koi majboori nahi."

05 Actions Over Words

"Bas... itna chahta hoon ki is baar tum mujhe mere lafzon se nahi, mere amal se pehchaano. Main tumhari khamoshi mein zabardasti apna naam nahi chhapna chahta; main khud ko itna badalna chahta hoon, itna paak aur sachcha banna chahta hoon ki ek din tumhein mere saamne khud ko mehfooz rakhne ke liye ek pal ke liye bhi sochna na pade. Main tumse yeh vaada nahi karunga ki tumhara patthar dil raaton-raat pighal hi jaayega, kyunki tumhare jazbaat par ab mera koi haq nahi bacha. Main bas yeh aakhri vaada karta hoon—is baar tumhein meri mohabbat ka bojh nahi, sirf uska sukoon mehsoos hoga. Aur agar kabhi, kisi din, tumhara yeh khali dil dobara mera naam le, toh woh kisi darr, guilt ya purani yaadon ke bojh tale nahi... balki is pure yakeen ke saath le ki jis insaan ne kabhi tumhara sab kuch tod diya tha, usne aakhirkaar tumhare liye jeena aur marna sikh liya."

"Main tumhein wapas paane se pehle... woh behtareen insaan banna chahta hoon, jiske paas aane ke baad tumhein kabhi khud ko bachane ki zaroorat hi na pade."
